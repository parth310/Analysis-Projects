# 🍽️ Swiggy Data Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Swiggy food delivery data to extract meaningful business insights and build an interactive dashboard. The goal is to understand customer preferences, restaurant performance, pricing patterns, and city-wise demand.

---

## 🎯 Objective

* Analyze food ordering patterns
* Identify top-performing restaurants and cities
* Understand pricing vs rating behavior
* Build an interactive dashboard for decision-making

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

* Power BI (Dashboard & Visualization)
* Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning)
* SQL (Optional Analysis)

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 KPI Metrics

* Total Orders
* Average Rating
* Average Price
* Total Restaurants

### 🔹 Interactive Filters (Slicers)

* City
* Food Category
* Veg / Non-Veg
* Week / Day

### 🔹 Visualizations

1. Top Restaurants by Rating
2. Category-wise Order Distribution
3. Orders by City
4. Price vs Rating Analysis (Scatter Plot)
5. Weekly Order Trends
6. Veg vs Non-Veg Comparison

---

## 📈 Key Insights

### 1. Customer Preference

Non-vegetarian items dominate the order volume, indicating higher demand for protein-rich meals.

### 2. City-wise Performance

Major cities contribute the highest number of orders, showing strong urban demand.

### 3. Price vs Rating

There is no strong correlation between price and rating. Affordable items often receive high ratings.

### 4. Restaurant Performance

A few restaurants consistently maintain high ratings and high order volume, acting as market leaders.

### 5. Category Insights

Fast food and popular categories like biryani dominate the demand.

### 6. Weekly Trends

Order volume fluctuates weekly, with peaks likely during weekends.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Promote high-rated low-price items
* Focus on top-performing cities for marketing
* Partner with high-performing restaurants
* Improve low-rated dishes using feedback
* Provide offers in low-performing categories
